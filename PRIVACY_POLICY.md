# PlanToSim Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2025

PlanToSim does not collect, store, or share personal information.

All flight plan processing happens locally on your iOS device.

When you send a plan to the PlanToSim Plugin, it is transmitted over your local network directly to your Windows PC — no data is sent to any external servers.

Apple may provide anonymized crash or analytics data if you have chosen to share it with developers in iOS settings. This data is used only to diagnose issues and improve the app.

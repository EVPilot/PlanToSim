How to Use PlanToSim

Step 1: Configure Settings

  1. Open PlanToSim
  2. Tap the Settings icon (gear) in the top left
  3. Enter your X-Plane IP Address (e.g., 192.168.1.100)
  4. Toggle on the navigator formats you want to export:
    • Reality-XP GTN - For Reality-XP GTN 750/650
    • TDS GTN - For TDS GTN Xi hardware
    • Reality-XP GNS - For Reality-XP GNS 530/430
    • X-Plane FMS - For X-Plane native FMS
  5. Choose approach conversion preferences
  6. Tap Done

Step 2: Import Flight Plan

From ForeFlight:

  1. Open your flight plan in ForeFlight
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Select PlanToSim from the share sheet

From Garmin Pilot (PDF):

  1. Export your flight plan as PDF
  2. Open the PDF in Files app
  3. Long-press the PDF and select Share → PlanToSim

Step 3: Conversion Happens Automatically

PlanToSim will:

  • Process your flight plan using OCR (for PDFs) or direct parsing (for .fpl files)
  • Convert to your selected navigator formats
  • Transfer files to X-Plane on your local network
  • Show "Flight Plan Converted!" when complete

Step 4: Fly!

Your flight plan is now ready in your simulator's GPS navigator or X-Plane FMS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which EFB apps are supported?

A: PlanToSim supports:

  • ForeFlight - .fpl XML files
  • Garmin Pilot - PDF exports with OCR
  • FlyQ EFB - PDF exports with OCR
Q: Which GPS navigator formats can I export?

A: PlanToSim supports:

  • TDS GTN Xi - Native procedure format (.gfp)
  • Reality-XP GTN 750/650 - Fix-to-fix expansion (.gfp)
  • Reality-XP GNS 530/430 - XML format (.fpl)
  • X-Plane FMS - Native FMS format (.fms)
Q: How do I connect to X-Plane?

A:

  1. Ensure X-Plane is running on your computer
  2. Ensure your iPad/iPhone and X-Plane computer are on the same Wi-Fi network
  3. Find your X-Plane computer's IP address:
    • Mac: System Preferences → Network
    • Windows: Command Prompt → ipconfig
  4. Enter this IP address in PlanToSim Settings
  5. Grant local network permission when iOS asks
Q: What navigation data does PlanToSim use?

A: PlanToSim includes a comprehensive navigation database:

  • 72,127+ navigation fixes (waypoints)
  • 47,382+ airports with ICAO identifiers
  • 1,203 airways (high and low altitude)
  • 460 SIDs (Standard Instrument Departures)
  • 279 STARs (Standard Terminal Arrival Routes)
  • 1,129 instrument approaches

Data sources: FAA CIFP, FAA NASR, OurAirports

Important: For simulation use only - not for real-world navigation.

Q: Does PlanToSim collect my data?

A: No! PlanToSim:

  • Does not collect any personal information
  • Does not track usage or analytics
  • Processes everything locally on your device
  • Only connects to your X-Plane on local network
  • Has no cloud services or external servers

See our Privacy Policy for complete details.

Q: What if the local network permission doesn't appear?

A: Try these solutions:

  1. Delete PlanToSim and reinstall from App Store
  2. Reset permissions: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Reset Location & Privacy
  3. Ensure you're on iOS 14 or later
Q: Can I use PlanToSim without X-Plane?

A: PlanToSim requires X-Plane or a compatible flight simulator to receive the converted flight plans. The app converts the files but needs a destination to send them to.

Q: What iOS version do I need?

A: PlanToSim requires iOS 17.6 or later.

Troubleshooting

Issue: "Coordinate not found for waypoint"

Solution: This usually means the waypoint isn't in the navigation database. This can happen with:

  • Custom user waypoints - ForeFlight custom waypoints are supported
  • Very new waypoints not yet in the FAA CIFP data
  • International waypoints outside the FAA database

Issue: OCR misreading waypoint names

Solution: PlanToSim includes advanced OCR with 17,000+ aviation terms. If misreading occurs:

  • Try exporting from ForeFlight (.fpl) instead of PDF for more accurate results
  • Ensure PDF quality is high (not scanned or low resolution)

Issue: Flight plan not appearing in X-Plane

Solution: Check:

  • X-Plane is running before sending flight plan
  • IP address in Settings is correct
  • iPhone/iPad and X-Plane computer are on same Wi-Fi network
  • Firewall isn't blocking port 5000
  • Local network permission was granted to PlanToSim

Issue: App crashes or doesn't respond

Solution:

  1. Force quit the app (swipe up from app switcher)
  2. Restart your iPhone/iPad
  3. Update to the latest version from App Store
  4. If problem persists, contact support
